n8n recipes

How the disconnected pieces become one product

These are the first four agency paths. JSON exports live in n8n/workflows/ on GitHub. Import them after the core profile is up.

01 · 25 min · Trigger: WordPress or native form POST

Form → Twenty contact → Mautic segment

  • Form hits n8n webhook.
  • Normalize name, email, phone, source.
  • Upsert person in Twenty GraphQL.
  • Create or update Mautic contact and add to a segment.
  • Open a Chatwoot conversation if the lead asked a question.

02 · 20 min · Trigger: New Twenty person with source = ads

Speed-to-lead SMS in 60 seconds

  • Twenty webhook → n8n.
  • Quiet hours check.
  • Twilio SMS with first-name merge.
  • Log the message on the contact.
  • If no reply in 10 minutes, create a Chatwoot assignment.

03 · 15 min · Trigger: BOOKING_CREATED

Cal.com booking → pipeline + reminder

  • Match attendee email to Twenty.
  • Move opportunity to Booked.
  • Mautic: enroll in appointment nurture.
  • n8n wait until T-24h, send SMS reminder.

04 · 40 min · Trigger: Conversation opened

Chatwoot sidebar shows CRM context

  • Lookup Twenty by email or phone.
  • Post a private note: deals, last campaign, tags.
  • If unknown, create a lead and tag source = inbox.
